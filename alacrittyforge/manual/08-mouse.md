# Mouse and links

The pointer, and opening links from the screen. In **Settings ▸ Mouse & links**.

## The settings

- **Hide the pointer while typing**: it comes back when you move the mouse.
- **Letters for link labels**: press **Ctrl+Shift+O** and Alacritty labels every link on screen; type a label to open it. This picks the letters the labels use.

Alacritty names on this page: `mouse.hide_when_typing`, `hints.alphabet`.
