# Cursor

The mark where you type. In **Settings ▸ Cursor**.

## The settings

- **Shape**: **Block**, **Underline** or **Beam** (a thin bar).
- **Blinking**: **Never** and **Always** can't be changed by programs; **Off** and **On** are where it starts, and a program may change them.
- **Blink speed**: the time between blinks, in milliseconds. Smaller is faster.
- **Stop blinking after**: stop after this many seconds without typing. **0** blinks forever.
- **Hollow when not in use**: show an empty box while another window has the focus.
- **Thickness**: how thick a Beam or Underline cursor is, as a share of a character's width.

Alacritty names on this page: `cursor.style.shape`, `cursor.style.blinking`, `cursor.blink_interval`, `cursor.blink_timeout`, `cursor.unfocused_hollow`, `cursor.thickness`.
